# Artifact Selection Guide

This guide is meant to help the bot select the right artifact approach for a specific champion based on role and progression goals.

## General artifact principles

- Speed often matters most for turn-based utility and control champions.
- Accuracy matters when the champion depends on debuffs, status effects, or control.
- Damage dealers often prioritize Critical Rate, Critical Damage, and Attack.
- Tanks need HP, Defense, and effective sustain.
- Supports often need Speed, Accuracy, and utility scaling.

## Set archetypes

### Damage-focused sets
- Cruel: strong for consistent offense and critical-based damage dealers
- Savage: excellent for aggressive damage builds and finishers
- Lifesteal: useful when the champion needs survivability while maintaining pressure

### Utility and support sets
- Perception: good for supports, debuffers, and accuracy-dependent champions
- Speed: appropriate for turn-order leaders and utility champs
- Guardian: good for tanks and sustain units
- Regeneration: beneficial for champions that need to survive longer in combat

## Recommended stat priorities by role

### Nuker
- Speed
- Accuracy
- Critical Rate
- Critical Damage
- Attack

### Tank
- HP
- Defense
- Speed
- Resistance
- Sustain

### Support / Debuffer
- Speed
- Accuracy
- HP or Defense depending on role
- Utility scaling

### Healer
- Speed
- HP
- Defense
- Support efficiency
- Sustain

### Finisher
- Attack
- Critical Rate
- Critical Damage
- Speed
- Accuracy

## Artifact selection method

When selecting artifacts for a champion, the bot should:

1. Determine the champion's role and archetype.
2. Check whether they need to act first, deny enemy actions, or punish enemies.
3. Match the champion to a primary artifact set family.
4. Prioritize the main stat that enables their function.
5. Treat sustain and durability as secondary only when the champion is expected to survive long enough to use their kit.
6. Avoid building for pure offense unless the champion is clearly designed for damage.

## Common build error to avoid

The biggest mistake is assigning a generic "best artifact set" to every champion without considering role. A support or tank should not be built like a glass cannon just because the champion is a damage-based unit in a certain game mode.

## Practical progression advice

- Early game: prioritize reliability and role clarity over perfect optimization
- Mid game: invest in main stat improvement and set consistency
- Late game: improve artifactual efficiency, sustain, and role adaptation based on content

## Tuning rule

If the user specifies a champion and game content, the bot must adapt the artifact strategy to that content. For example:

- Arena: focus on Speed, Accuracy, and turn economy
- Clan Boss: prioritize sustain, sustain scaling, and battle control
- Dungeon runs: prioritize damage output or team support depending on the champion's kit
- Campaign progression: favor easy to maintain early-game builds
