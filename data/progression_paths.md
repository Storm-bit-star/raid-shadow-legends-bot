# Progression Path Planning

This file gives the bot a framework for recommending how a player should progress their account and team.

## Core progression principles

1. Build a core team that works across multiple stages.
2. Prioritize a stable speed lead, support, sustain unit, and damage carry.
3. Keep progression flexible: do not overcommit to one lane too early.
4. Upgrade champions in a practical order based on actual account needs.

## Early game progression

- Prioritize champions that help with campaign and early dungeons
- Focus on clear role assignments: one damage unit, one support unit, one sustain unit
- Use artifacts that improve consistency and survival instead of a fully optimized late-game build
- Build a small team that can clear content without specialized gearing

## Mid game progression

- Improve speed and initiative order
- Begin focusing on stat priorities for each role
- Add more specialized champions for key content like dungeons and arena
- Continue moving toward stable artifact sets and better utility

## Late game progression

- Make sure key champions are built around their real purpose
- Add meaningful support and sustain to difficult content
- Adjust artifact decisions based on the exact dungeon or boss being cleared
- Prioritize team composition and battle rhythm over individual stat spikes

## Recommended general order

1. Build a basic core team for farming and campaign progression
2. Add a damage carry
3. Add a support or debuffer
4. Add a tank or sustain-focused unit
5. Improve utility and niche mission coverage
6. Expand into more specialized late-game team roles

## Team-building template

A good general team often includes:

- One champion acting as the speed lead or turn-order enabler
- One or two damage dealers
- One tank or sustain leader
- One utility or control champion

The exact composition may vary by champion and content, but the underlying principle stays the same: support the team with role clarity and consistent progression.

## Assessment questions the bot should ask

- What champion do you want to build?
- What stage is your account in?
- Are you focusing on early campaign, arena, dungeons, or clan boss?
- Do you want a more offensive, supportive, or balanced build?
- Do you have a limited artifact set pool or a large progression budget?

## Recommendation output

The bot should combine the above progression logic with champion-specific role analysis to produce a build plan that is realistic for that account stage and content goal.
